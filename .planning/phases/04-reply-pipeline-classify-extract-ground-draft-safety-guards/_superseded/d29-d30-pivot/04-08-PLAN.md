removing or weakening any existing trigger (low_confidence, high_risk_category, conflict, stale_only,

and change_request → escalate when the draft would falsely claim an operational action (RD-Q1, Phase-1
"answers customers only" constraint). The escalation gate is the deterministic enforcer of those rows.
Output: extended escalation_gate.py + a dedicated test proving the new trigger blocks and the old ones survive.
