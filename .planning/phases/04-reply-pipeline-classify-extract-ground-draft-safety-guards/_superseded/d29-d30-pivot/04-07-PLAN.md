<objective>
Extend the classifier to emit a level-2 `customer_request` sub-type (the 13-value RULES §2 enum) alongside
the existing macro `category`, `confidence`, and `high_risk` fields. This makes the per-sub-type rule table
(RULES §2) addressable by escalation_gate (04-08), pre_send_guard (04-09), and the drafter (04-10).

Purpose: Without the level-2 sub-type, the guard and escalation gate cannot tell Return from Review from
Cancel_Order — and the whole authorized-offer rework is unaddressable. This plan adds exactly that field,
keeps Haiku (D-03), and preserves the additive verdict-schema contract for Phase 5.
Output: updated classifier agent + classify-ticket skill + a structural contract test.
</objective>
